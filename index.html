DailyPal Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

What you enter in DailyPal never leaves your device.

DailyPal is a task planner for Android and iOS. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your data stays on your device

Your tasks, time logs and settings are stored only on your device. DailyPal has no accounts, servers or advertising, and nothing you enter in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

Usage and crash data (optional)

On Android you can choose to share anonymous usage and crash data to help us improve DailyPal. It is off unless you turn it on, during setup or in Settings, and you can turn it off again at any time.

If you turn it on, the app sends this to Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics), which processes it for us:

  • Usage events: which screens and buttons you use, and rough counts such as how many tasks you have or a range a task's estimate falls in.
  • Crash reports: what went wrong in the app and where in its code.
  • Device and app details: device model, operating system and app version, language, approximate country (Google derives it from your IP address and does not keep the address), and a random ID for this app installation.

It never includes your task names, notes, search text, or the dates and times you enter. DailyPal does not use your advertising ID, and the data is not used for advertising, not combined with other Google data about you, and never sold.

We use it only to find and fix bugs and to see which features are worth improving. Usage data is kept for 2 months and crash reports for 90 days. Turning sharing off stops it at once, resets the installation ID and deletes crash reports not yet sent. Our legal basis is your consent. Google's handling is described in the Firebase privacy information and Google's privacy policy; Google may process the data outside your country under its standard safeguards.

Notifications and the home-screen widget

Reminders and the Android home-screen widget are computed on your device from your own tasks. The app asks for these permissions:

  • Notifications, to remind you about tasks that are due, at risk or overdue. You can turn each type off in Settings, or deny the permission entirely.
  • Run at startup (Android), so reminders keep working after your device restarts.

Feedback

Sending feedback is optional. The suggestion board linked from the app opens in your browser and is hosted by Featurebase, whose own privacy policy applies there. If you post there or email us, we can see what you write and the name or email address you use. We use this only to reply to you and to improve DailyPal. We never use it for marketing, and we never sell or share it. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by asking us to delete your messages.

Device backups

If you have turned on backups on your device (for example Google backup or iCloud), your device may include DailyPal's data in them. Your device and backup provider control this, not us.

Deleting your data

Uninstall DailyPal or clear its storage to erase everything the app holds on your device. Turn off "Share usage and crash data" in Settings to stop sharing and reset your installation ID; data already sent is deleted automatically after the periods above. To have feedback you sent us deleted, email us at the address below.

Your rights

For any feedback or email you have sent us, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. For usage and crash data, you can withdraw your consent at any time with the switch in Settings. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

DailyPal is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If DailyPal ever starts collecting anything more, we will update this page and ask you in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact
