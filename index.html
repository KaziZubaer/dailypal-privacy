DailyPal Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

DailyPal does not collect your personal data.

DailyPal is a task planner for Android and iOS. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your data stays on your device

Your tasks, time logs and settings are stored only on your device. DailyPal has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking, and nothing you enter in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

Notifications and the home-screen widget

Reminders and the Android home-screen widget are computed on your device from your own tasks. The app asks for these permissions:

  • Notifications, to remind you about tasks that are due, at risk or overdue. You can turn each type off in Settings, or deny the permission entirely.
  • Run at startup (Android), so reminders keep working after your device restarts.

Feedback

Sending feedback is optional. The suggestion board linked from the app opens in your browser and is hosted by Featurebase, whose own privacy policy applies there. If you post there or email us, we can see what you write and the name or email address you use. We use this only to reply to you and to improve DailyPal. We never use it for marketing, and we never sell or share it. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by asking us to delete your messages.

Device backups

If you have turned on backups on your device (for example Google backup or iCloud), your device may include DailyPal's data in them. Your device and backup provider control this, not us.

Deleting your data

Uninstall DailyPal or clear its storage to erase everything the app holds. To have feedback you sent us deleted, email us at the address below.

Your rights

For any feedback or email you have sent us, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

DailyPal is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If DailyPal ever starts handling your data differently, we will update this page and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests: dailypal.support@gmail.com
